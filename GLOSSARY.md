# Fussball

Fussball records player and team matches and maintains the Elo ratings derived from match history.

## Access

**Administrator**:
The person responsible for maintaining player and match records.

## Competition statistics

**Win rate**:
The proportion of a player's recorded matches that the player won, expressed as a percentage of all matches played.
_Avoid_: Winrate

**Team ranking**:
The competition ranking of two-player teams based on their latest recorded rating; teams with equal current ratings share a rank. Single-player teams remain part of rating history but are not shown in the Team Rankings list.
